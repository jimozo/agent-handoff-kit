# Code Of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Public or private abuse.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful.
